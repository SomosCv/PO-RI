Install Prevent Overdose RI
Add this app to your home screen for faster access, offline support, and a native app experience.
📱 iPhone / iPad
1. Open this site in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap Add.
🤖 Android
1. Tap the browser menu (⋮).
2. Tap Install App or Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm installation.
💻 Desktop
1. Look for the install icon in your browser address bar.
2. Click Install.
3. Launch the app from your desktop or applications menu.
